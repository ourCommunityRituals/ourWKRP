## The end goal

Not a million dollars.

Family comfortable. Health care not a worry.
Passive income reached.

After that threshold — the experiment becomes
the point. The methodology gets published.
The proof is in the archive.

Social science and STEM get taken more seriously
working together. Hyperlocal economies that own
their own data, document their own vitality,
and walk into city council hearings with three
years of evidence.

That is the pushback.
Not a protest. Not a petition.
Data that cannot be argued against because
the community generated it voluntarily
and owns it by design.

## NCR Methodology

Node Corridor Ring.

The published academic methodology underlying
the platform. The geographic experiment running
simultaneously with the business.

Node:     The specific activation point.
Corridor: The organic geographic thread
          connecting nodes. The can span across, through, up to, agaisnt, bypass, etc node by what the geography, culture, and econonmic behavior dictate
Ring:     The self-sustaining local economic loop of the varying levels of      nodes and corridors permenating the economic geography.

Six-layer geographic hierarchy from hyperlocal
to macro region. Ring closes when the corridor
economy no longer depends on outside investment.

See LOCKED_NCR_Methodology_v1.3 for full doctrine.
