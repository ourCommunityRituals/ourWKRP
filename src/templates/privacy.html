Privacy

What ourWKRP keeps about you, and what you can do about it

The short version

  • Your profile is public, by your name. That is the point of ourWKRP: you plant your flag.
  • You can download everything we keep about you, and delete your profile, at any time from your own profile page.
  • We use one sign-in cookie. No advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools.
  • You must be 16 or older to make a profile.

Who we are

ourWKRP is run by OurCommunityRituals LLC. Contact: privacy@oursharedevents.com.

What we keep and why

  • Your profile: the name you give, your page address, your profile type, the domains you declare, your communities, how your page is arranged, and whether people may vote for you. Shown publicly, because you chose to make a profile.
  • Music you bring (only when music is switched on): your playlists and the tracks you added. Linked Spotify or Last.fm accounts keep their sign-in tokens only for syncing.
  • Sign-in: a random session id, so you stay signed in.
  • Security log: what was done to your profile and when, under your profile's id. It never holds your name or email.
  • Abuse protection: a scrambled form of your IP address, kept for about a minute to stop floods of requests. The address itself is not stored.

Legal basis: your consent when you make a profile (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure (Art. 6(1)(f)).

How long we keep it

  • Your profile: until you delete it.
  • Sign-in: a session ends after 7 days without use, and after 30 days at most.
  • Security log: one year.
  • Abuse protection: about one minute.

Cookies

  • wkrp_session — keeps you signed in. Set only when you make a profile or sign in. It lasts up to 30 days and is removed when you sign out or delete your profile.

That is the only cookie. It is needed for signing in, so we do not ask first.

Who handles it for us

  • Cloudflare, Inc. runs the website and stores the data, under its Data Processing Addendum.
  • If you link Spotify or Last.fm, they handle what you share with them under their own privacy policies.

Cloudflare is based in the United States. Transfers are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and by standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

Your rights

  • See and download your data (GDPR Art. 15 and 20): on your profile page, choose "Download my data".
  • Delete your profile (Art. 17): on your profile page, choose "Delete my profile". Your name and page address are removed and you are signed out. Tracks you added stay in the shared catalog, with no link to you.
  • Correct (Art. 16), object (Art. 21) or withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)): email us. We answer within one month.
  • Complain to your data protection supervisory authority. In the EU, find yours at edpb.europa.eu. In the UK, the ICO.

Children

ourWKRP is not for children. You must be 16 or older to make a profile. If you think a child has made one, email us and we will delete it.
